# Freight Analysis Framework

BTS [page](https://www.bts.gov/faf)

> The Freight Analysis Framework (FAF) creates a comprehensive picture of
> freight movement among states and major metropolitan areas by all modes of
> transportation. The FAF integrates data from a variety of sources. Starting
> with data from the Commodity Flow Survey (CFS) and international trade data
> from the Census Bureau, FAF incorporates data from agriculture, extraction,
> utility, construction, service, and other sectors. The FAF is produced
> through a partnership between BTS and FHWA.
